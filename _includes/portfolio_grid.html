How It Works

Putting the 54 Nations Code into action in schools

Identify

Teachers pinpoint the specific country or culture being studied rather than generalizing “Africa.”

Research

Lessons use accurate, context-rich resources to reflect each nation’s history, achievements, and culture.

Teach

Deliver lessons in a way that avoids stereotypes and actively rewrites the narrative about African countries.

Share

Schools share examples or feedback to help spread best practices and inspire others.
