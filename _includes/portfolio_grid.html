Start using the 54 Nations Code

Empower your school to implement the 54 Nations Code and transform how African nations are taught.

1

Audit Curriculum

Carefully review your current lessons to identify where African countries are grouped together or inaccurately represented. Take note of textbooks, activities, and discussions that need updating to reflect each nation’s uniqueness.

2

Revise Content

Update materials to highlight the unique history, culture, and achievements of each African nation. Include diverse examples, context, and accurate facts so students can understand the continent’s richness.

3

Share & Inspire

Document your school’s progress and share best practices with others. Encourage fellow educators to join the 54 Nations Code movement and spread awareness about individual African nations.
