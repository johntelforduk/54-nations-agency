What We Aim to Change

Transforming education about Africa with the 54 Nations Code

Specificity

Encourage teachers and curricula to refer to individual countries, cultures, and regions, rather than treating Africa as a single entity.

Accuracy

Ensure all teaching materials and discussions are factually correct, context-rich, and reflect the true diversity of African nations.

Narrative

Promote a positive and accurate portrayal of African nations, actively countering stereotypes and rewriting the narrative in classrooms.
