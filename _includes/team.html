Behind 54 Nations

Why I created the 54 Nations Code.

I started the 54 Nations Code after noticing how often Africa was spoken about in school as if it were one place, through phrases like “African history” or “African culture.” As a continent of 54 countries, each with its own identity, this lack of specificity felt both inaccurate and limiting. What began as a simple question about why this happens grew into a determination to create something that could change it. Behind this initiative, is just one South-London school girl, with a vision and hope for changing the tought perception of Africa.
